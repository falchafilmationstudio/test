ABOUT

STORYTELLING IN MOTION

A "Falcha" (फल्चा) in traditional Newar architecture is an open public pavilion where travelers rest, community elders converse, and generations have shared folk tales under carved wooden eaves.

Falcha Filmation Studio was founded on this very philosophy: an open creative haven where authentic stories from the heart of Nepal meet contemporary cinematic craft.
